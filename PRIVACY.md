# Privacy Policy

**Observed collects no data. None.**

## What the app stores

Everything you log is stored locally on your device using SwiftData, in the
app's own sandboxed container. It never leaves your device.

## Network access

The app contains no networking code. It makes no HTTP requests, contacts no
servers, and includes no third-party SDKs, analytics, advertising, or crash
reporting frameworks.

## What is collected

Nothing. No account is required. No identifiers are generated or transmitted.
No usage statistics are gathered. There is nothing to opt out of because
nothing is collected.

## Third parties

None. The app uses only Apple's own frameworks (SwiftUI, SwiftData, Foundation).

## Deleting your data

Delete a habit and its entire occurrence history is removed with it. Delete the
app and all data is gone permanently. Because nothing is stored off-device,
there is no server-side copy to request or erase.

## Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy ever changes, the change will be documented in
[CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md). Given the design of the app, it is unlikely to.
